## Administrator reference review

- [Navigating Axiom](https://community.veracross.com/s/article/Navigating-Axiom): persistent Launchpad, department homepages, search, Add and Related Pages.
- [The Axiom Launchpad](https://community.veracross.com/s/article/The-Axiom-Launchpad): grouped homepages, favorites, history and quick access to records.
- [Adding Courses](https://community.veracross.com/s/article/Adding-Courses): a course is a year-independent curriculum record; classes hold the teachers and school year. General fields include type, subject, course ID/name and reporting flags.
- [Scheduler Tune-Up](https://community.veracross.com/s/article/Scheduler-Tune-Up-and-Quick-Start-Guide): year-specific classes, blocks and rooms are scheduling prerequisites.

This pass keeps Cedar Ridge branding and existing shared school records. It improves navigation, record density, registrations, filtering, and visibility of generated years/terms. It does not claim access to the vendor's private administrator application, implement its entire product, or migrate browser-local records to a hosted service.
